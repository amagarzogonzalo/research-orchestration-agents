# Rubric: generic document

Score each criterion 1-10 against the anchors. Quote evidence before scoring.
Do not reward length, fluency, or confidence.

## 1. Claim support
- **9-10** Every non-obvious claim traces to a cited source, pointing at the passage
  that supports it rather than a homepage.
- **5-6** Most claims cited, but a load-bearing one rests on a source that does not say it.
- **1-2** Citations are decorative: present, unconnected to the claims.

## 2. Source discrimination
- **9-10** Primary and authoritative sources preferred; secondary coverage used only to
  characterize reception, and labeled as such.
- **5-6** Mixes primary and aggregator sources without distinguishing them.
- **1-2** Leans on content farms or reproduces a single secondary source.

## 3. Handling of the unknown
- **9-10** States where evidence is thin or contested, and what would settle it.
- **5-6** Hedges vaguely without naming the gap.
- **1-2** Presents contested or missing material as settled.

## 4. Structure serves the reader
- **9-10** Order follows the question's logic; one fact is findable without reading it all.
- **5-6** Generic intro/body/conclusion imposed on unsuited material.
- **1-2** Sections restate each other, or the answer is buried.

## 5. No padding
- **9-10** Every paragraph carries information the previous one did not.
- **5-6** One or two paragraphs restate the thesis in new words.
- **1-2** Substantial restatement or listicle bloat.

`overall` is the MINIMUM of the five scores, not the mean. A document with one
broken leg does not average out to standing.
